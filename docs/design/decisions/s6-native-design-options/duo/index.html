Decision 1: unfolded deck layout

The first capture in each option is the inner display. Folded, A and B keep the same deck page and tabs shown here under A.

A. Sidebar and deck page

Closest to your Mac app and to the current requirement. The sidebar is about a third of the screen, and there's no list-only view of your decks. The switch from side tabs to a sidebar is the biggest visual change when you unfold.

B. Deck list and deck, Settings behind a toggle

Like Mail on the Duo, with one more level of hierarchy on the big screen. The list gets more room. Settings takes one extra tap. Half open, the system moved the deck page clear of the fold by itself, as the second capture shows. The divider sits at about 418 points, a little short of the fold.

Decision 2: review

Outer display on the left, half open on the right, with the fold marked in red. None of these has a control on the fold, and the card stayed loaded through folding in B and C.

A. Answers either side of the fold

Recommended

The same four buttons in the same order, always at the bottom, so your thumbs learn one layout. Unfolded, Again and Hard sit under the left thumb, Good and Easy under the right. Show Answer is wider and heavier than the answers, the one thing here I'd still slim down.

C. Answer column at the side

The same place on both displays, within reach of one right thumb. The card can use the full width when unfolded and may cross the fold. Folded, the card loses about 96 points of a 466-point screen, and left-handed use is worse.
