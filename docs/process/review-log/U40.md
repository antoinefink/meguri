### A1. The day and unbury scenarios' Meguri runs move from U40 to U28

- Before: U40's Verification asked for every `conformance/scenarios/sched/day/` and `sched/unbury-on-rollover/` golden to match on Meguri's executor, macOS native and Linux Docker.
- Why U40 cannot meet it: T0 on unit/U40 before this round (`tools/mg conformance`, both lanes) reports all 35 of them (21 day scenarios, the 11 generated matrix files and 3 unbury scenarios) pending: 32 for `setup: new_collection` alone, 2 for `setup: config, new_collection`, and `day/legacy-timing-after-answer` also for `answer`, `notetypes` and `notes` (A2).
  - `new_collection` is the new collection of `specs/conformance/dsl.md` D6 and D8: U28's bootstrap (`storage/new-collection`, on spec/U28), or a stand-in an area's spec defines. U28 depends on U40: its `crt` and `creationOffset` come from U40's new-collection timing (R596, `sched/day-clock` D11). The one stand-in a spec defines, U29's (`notetype-ops` T10.2, on spec/U29), is the storage create path with the stock kinds and does not say how it sets `crt` or `creationOffset`. Registering a U40 stand-in would mean a spec for it that does not exist, and a second `Registry::new_collection` that T10.2's would collide with.
- Done in U40 (round 1): the testkit's `setup.config` adapter (`dsl.md` D6.1, written as `set_config` writes, W4), so the two scenarios that use it now wait for the new collection only. With the `timing` observation and the `reopen` op, Meguri's executor has everything the day scenarios use but the new collection. `core/crates/meguri-engine/tests/day_conformance.rs` plays every day scenario (the generated matrix included) but `legacy-timing-after-answer`, and every unbury scenario, on the engine's day clock against its golden, with a copy of the schema-18 fixture standing in for the new collection.
- Now: U40's Verification asks for that test, the adapters and no core timer. U28's Verification asks for the 34 scenarios on Meguri's executor on both lanes, listed in `conformance/scenarios/sched/area.yaml` (added with `active: []`).
- Reversing A1 means building U28's bootstrap, or having a spec writer define a day-area stand-in that U29's T10.2 then extends, inside U40.
- Not done, considered: having the spec writer rewrite the day scenarios to open the schema-18 fixture (as `search/errors/run` does) and the oracle maintainer re-record them, so they run now. The `creation-*` scenarios check the new collection itself (D11), the first day query at creation is part of what D14.2 checks, and the fixture holds buried cards and timing keys of its own; it would trade coverage for an earlier run.
