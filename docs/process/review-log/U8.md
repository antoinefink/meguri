### Round 2 (2026-10-13, at 2feeb81; fixed in 510851d, 8cbccbf and e453657)

Codex lens (three P1) and conformance lens (one P1, three P2, three P3). Fixer session s-20261014T053819Z-76be1490.

- P1, a recording's hand-over could lose the host lease to a half-made nested slot. Fixed: every lease change except taking a free lease runs under one flock mutex (`tools/scripts/host-lease-mutex.sh`).

Mac runs with dropped input now end `blocked: mac-input` (error, user step mac-input), not fail.
- P2, xcode-check launched the app without the host lease. Fixed: the builds run without the lease, and the launches and test runs run under it.
- P2, a failed status-bar override was ignored. Fixed: it ends the job error.
- P2, no test showed that two worktrees share one lease. Added: a bats test with a real git worktree and `MEGURI_HOST_LEASE_DIR` unset.
- P3 items: all three are still open (items 5, 6 and 7 below).
