## Round 1 (fixer s-20261015T155147Z-cf16c51a; fixed in e9b692a, cfc6090, 88f2c1b and b4a9467)

- `SwapRecorder` made a sample of an unconfirmed swap only when the next swap began, so a missing R1862 confirmation on a session's last swap was never counted. `finish()` (the session-end flush U176 calls) now turns every waiting swap into a miss, in swap order (e9b692a).
- `FieldDiagnosticsStore` and its subscriber ran on the main actor: every MetricKit delivery read, decoded and wrote files on the UI thread (KTD9), and each report rewrote the whole signpost history. The store is now an actor on its own utility queue, and files are only added (cfc6090).
- R1864's Apple-silicon numbers (10k, 100k, 150k, 500k, phys_footprint) could not come from `tools/mg`: `bench` runs only in the dev container. New `bench-mac` runs the criterion suites with host cargo under the host lease (`--no-session`, so a locked Mac runs it) (88f2c1b).
- `PerfTestCase` warmed no web content process (R1866), and `perf-ui` passed a performance suite that recorded no metric. Hook builds now answer `warmWebContent`, `measure(app:)` waits for it, and `perf-compare` refuses a suite with no metrics or a declared interval with no measurement (b4a9467).

## Round 2 (fixer s-20261015T165225Z-cc4609c6; fixed in 2cc29bc)

- A field diagnostics report written straight to its final name could be left partial when the app stopped mid-write, and every later export then failed. Reports now go to a `.tmp` file and are hard-linked to a unique final name. Reports that do not decode are skipped and kept.
- The merge of feat/meguri-v1 (3e5af5d, U8c's VM refactor) kept perf-ui's test plan and scheme in the VM xctestrun lookup and bench-mac's `--no-session` lease.

## Round 3 (fixer s-20261015T171556Z-8a3cb01a; fixed in 558a9f6)

- After U8c, `perf-ui` and `metrics` still took the host lease and never called `mg_sim_begin` or `mg_sim_take`. Their device checks refused the VM's simulator (runtime build mismatch), and `metrics` started xctrace on this Mac. Both jobs now follow the simulator-job order. A VM simulator gets its own baseline key (`vm/<name>/<model>/<build>`). `metrics` runs xctrace in the VM, stops it through a pid file and cleans it up on exit. The default template is Logging, because Time Profiler does not start in the VM.

## Round 4 (fixer s-20261015T181646Z-4d5ff3ae; fixed in d90024d)

The job now kills the stalled xctrace and its waiter, confirms both are gone, and starts xctrace once more (`details.xctrace.starts`). The cause is in Instruments: DTServiceHub crashes in `authorized_ktrace_start_fd` (7 of 10 Logging starts on 2026-10-15), and the retry does not make `metrics` reliable in the VM. `tools/AGENTS.md` records this.
