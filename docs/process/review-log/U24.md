## Round 1 (fixer s-20261015T214054Z-14a7e34d)

Three defects, all fixed. Each fix has a regression test that failed before it.

- c690e0e: a reader request checked the handle's generation before it waited for the reader. A replacement that finished in between gave the old handle a reader of the new collection. `Readers::lease` now runs the check with the reader's lock held (lifecycle L3).
- 4e8ec09: a long-operation slot guard outlived its collection. After close A and open B, A's guard could take B's core, and A's restore could replace B. A guard taken with nothing open could take the next collection. A close during an exclusive phase left `replace()` waiting forever on a stopped writer. The slot now records its generation and close frees it. The exclusive phase checks the slot, the core and the state again before a replace and on drop. The executor refuses all tasks once the writer stops (lifecycle L5, L7, L10).
- e94a50d: the event dispatcher subscribed to the change stream only on the first `set_event_listener()` or `publish()`. In a fresh process, a job that ended before Swift attached its listener published `JobFinished` to nobody (change-stream S3). The runtime now creates the engine and the dispatcher in one init.

## Round 2 (fixer s-20261015T220746Z-bc71aebf)

One defect, fixed in 5df5777. 04e9bb3 merged feat/meguri-v1 first.

- With `synchronous=NORMAL`, nothing synced a returned commit's WAL frames. With a reader holding a snapshot, no checkpoint made it durable either, so a power loss could drop changes already reported as saved. The writer thread now does the C9.2 flush: a full sync of the `-wal` itself (`Writer::sync_wal`, never a checkpoint), due 750 ms after a change, outside every write phase. A long task gets the flush first, and steady traffic cannot starve it. A failed sync is logged and retried with backoff. Tests: `core/crates/meguri-engine/tests/wal_durability.rs`.

### Round 1 (codex lens: plan conformance, correctness; fixer s-20261016T053150Z-37b9fd2a)

Three findings. Two fixed, one partly fixed.

- Finding 1 [P1], fixed in 13b9577: a swap that failed after it changed the file (for example, a failed directory sync after the rename) reopened the new file under the old generation. Nothing published `CollectionReplaced` or told the generation listeners, so old handles, readers and caches stayed valid against different data. The writer now notes the live file before the swap (the file the path names, its size, its modification and change times, its WAL size) and holds the old file open. When anything differs after the swap, the reopen moves to the next generation and publishes `CollectionReplaced`, and the replace still returns the swap's error. Lifecycle L8. Tests: `core/crates/meguri-engine/tests/lifecycle.rs` (a swap that fails after or before touching the file).
- Finding 2 [P1], fixed in 646c74f: the 150k reader test's 200-row page p95 was 19.3 ms in a workspace run (budget 16 ms). Alone, it is 0.7-2.2 ms. A nextest override (`core/.config/nextest.toml`) now runs every test named `*_at_150k` alone, and `core/AGENTS.md` records the naming rule. The budgets and the reader path are unchanged.
- Finding 3 [P2], partly fixed in 0144ca7: the 1,000-op test stopped at the observers and the engine subscription, and the ffi dispatcher dropped `Changes` and `CollectionReplaced` because `CoreEvent` had no variants for them. `CoreEventKind` now has both, the ffi's own publish refuses them, and CoreBridge maps them to a Swift `ChangeSet` and two `CoreEvent` cases. New tests: the dispatcher delivers an op's change set, a replacement, and 1,000 change sets reserved and filled from four threads, each once and in seq order; Swift receives a hook-published change set field for field.
