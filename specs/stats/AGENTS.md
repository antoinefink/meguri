# specs/stats/

- Two day rules, never derived from each other: Anki's day (`day-buckets` B2, B3: fixed 86,400 s spans back from `next_day_at`) for the Stats screen, studied today and searches; the heatmap day (B4: local date of the instant moved back by the rollover hours, offset at the moved instant) for heatmaps, streaks and the widget.
- The activity heatmap (`activity-heatmap`, contract N14.11) uses Anki's day (B3), not B4, by design, and deck pages' home-deck scope; it reads no `heatmap` key and keeps no resident aggregates.
- Heatmap rules follow the Review Heatmap add-on 1.0.1 (oracle: the add-on image), not Anki. The only deviations are R1280 (future rows on today), R1284 (deck pages by home deck), B4.6 (the add-on's calendar draws a DST-start date's count on the day before; Meguri does not) and `heatmap-addon` A15 item 10 (filtered cards' forecast by `odue`). Home's `limdecks` reads the current deck.
- Card Info dates are plain local calendar dates of the instant, with no rollover shift (`card-info` C2, C3.3, C5.1), not the heatmap day of B4.
- Day-0 edges differ on purpose: studied today counts `id > S`, the Stats Today section `id >= S`, graph day indices truncate toward zero.
- Two number writers, never shared (`studied-today` T5): the Home line rounds binary32 values and never groups digits (Anki's core; the oracle compares its text); every Stats-screen line rounds the shortest decimal of a binary64 value and groups with `,` (Anki's web page; no oracle text, tested from spec tables). Stats-screen text may first round with `graph-presentation` P2.1's L(p) (binary64 multiply, round, divide) before F writes it.
- Stats-screen deck scopes match `did` or `odid` in the subtree (`deck:` semantics); deck-page heatmaps use the home deck only.
- Graph ownership (`graphs-common` G12): scopes, history, row reading, aggregate shape and caches in `graphs-common`; bins, ranges, quantiles, labels and bar queries in `graph-presentation` (U226); scope text, join and fixed-item queries in `browse-queries`. Per-graph rules in `graphs-revlog`/`graphs-cards`.
- The Stats browse join is not canonical text: `(<scope>) AND <item>`, deck as `deck:"<escaped>"` (AE60 is byte-exact; Q2.2's names take `deck:"Current"`/`deck:"Filtered"` or `did:`). Never route it through the canonical writer or U88's `join`, whatever `search/join-and-replace` and the plan say (`browse-queries` intro).
- Graph `time` reads 0 outside `u32` (`model/row-records` R5); the Home line sums stored `time`. A card's missing `lrt` is never filled from the review log in graph data.
- Kind status lives in `conformance/stats-ops` (H4, H8 to H14), not here; `conformance/goldens/stats/` shows which scenarios have goldens. Planned, not in the tree: the `stats_presentation` scenarios (`graph-presentation` P23, `conformance/stats-ops` H14), `preferences-read` (H13), and W9's snapshot ones, which also need `widget_snapshot` and a Meguri-golden path. The `day-buckets`, `heatmap-totals`, `heatmap`, `streaks`, `heatmap-config` and `widget-today-count` scenarios are held: not in the tree, no goldens (a 2026-11-10 add-on probe was discarded). Test U95/U96/U100 against the B7, N9 and A16 tables until they land; never use the old copies on `spec/U95`.
